Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

RoR Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account and YouTube channel.

What it accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel.
  • Read-only access to that channel's details and its YouTube Analytics statistics.

How data is handled

  • The sign-in token and the statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or sent to any third party.
  • No data is collected from anyone other than the owner.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
